[SingleStore Helios](https://www.singlestore.com/cloud/) is the fully managed service for SingleStoreDB. Customers provision workspaces, load data, run queries, and get on with their day without running the database themselves.

## What I work on

I build product surfaces on Helios. Things that should feel fast and clear: data loading in the UI, search and navigation (we made Command+K more than 5× faster), and helpers like SQrL that turn what you want into SQL.

- [Command+K, more than 5× faster](https://www.singlestore.com/blog/enhancing-productivity-how-we-made-command-k-search-more-than-5x-faster/)
- [SQrL code generator](https://www.singlestore.com/blog/introducing-sqrl-code-generator/)

## Why it matters

A managed database only works if the cloud product is good. Helios is the path from “I have data” to “I can use SingleStore.” Calling them customers (not users) makes you want to try harder on the experience.
