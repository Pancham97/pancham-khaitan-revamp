[Aura Analyst](https://www.singlestore.com/ai/aura-analyst/) is SingleStore’s AI data analyst. Natural language in, governed SQL and insights out. It sits on Helios and Aura AI as a real product, not a demo chatbot.

## What I work on

I help build the foundations so agent-style analysis is accurate, context-aware, and safe enough for real data. Not novelty for its own sake. Answers people can act on.

## Why it matters

Enterprise AI is easy to make flashy and hard to make trustworthy. Aura Analyst aims for the hard part: conversational exploration with transparent SQL, so business folks and engineers are looking at the same ground truth.
